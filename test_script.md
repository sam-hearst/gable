# Gable usability test script

**Roles:** Host reads the script and stays quiet. Note-taker writes down pauses, wrong clicks, anything said out loud, and any task the tester gives up on. Don't help, even if they're stuck — that's the data.

**Before starting (consent):**
"Is it OK if we record your screen and voice? It's only for our team to review, and we'll delete it after the class." (Record only if they say yes.)

## Intro (read word for word)
"I'm going to have you try to use our product. Please think out loud: say whatever's on your mind. I won't help you. There are no wrong answers. We're testing the product, not you."

## Tasks
1. "Go to [STAGING URL](https://gable-git-staging-gable2.vercel.app/). Without clicking anything yet, what do you think this product does? Who is it for?"
2. "Please try to create an account."
3. "Imagine you have a summer internship in New York from June to August, and your budget is about $1,600 a month. Use Gable to find a place you'd actually consider."
4. "Save two or three places you like, then compare them."

## After (ask all three)
1. "When did you first understand what this is for?"
2. "What was the most confusing part?"
3. "What would have made it easier?"

Optional: "Would you use this for a real search? Why or why not?"
